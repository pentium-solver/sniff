# Supporting sniff! 🤝

**sniff!** is a movement to professionalize and democratize mobile reverse engineering. We are building a best-in-class analysis suite—a project defined by precision engineering, modular architecture, and full-stack intelligence.

We are looking for partners, creators, and platforms who share our vision for a more transparent and secure mobile ecosystem.

---

## 🏎️ Our Vision: Precision Tooling for RE
We believe the cybersecurity space is currently cluttered with fragmented, unreliable tools. **sniff!** is the antidote.

*   **Look & Feel:** A zero-latency, high-end web dashboard that feels like a modern IDE, not a terminal dump.
*   **Engineering:** A modular Go-based engine that treats Android, iOS, and Desktop as first-class citizens.
*   **Intelligence:** Built-in cryptographic signature reversal and automated OpenAPI documentation.
*   **Impact:** Collapsing hours of manual environment setup and packet analysis into a single, elegant click.
